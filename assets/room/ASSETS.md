# My Space furniture assets

Created with the built-in image_gen tool on 16 November 2026 for this app. The 12 individual transparent PNGs are original generated furniture, based on the project's own furniture-preview.png art-direction sheet. That sheet follows the user's requested warm neutral, fine-pixel isometric style. No third-party furniture sheet has been bundled as production art.

Original generated PNGs are retained unchanged. bounds.json measures their alpha bounds for runtime alignment; it does not change their pixels. RoomBackdrop and ActivityArt are original code-native geometry. Furniture supports two display orientations; the second is mirrored. It is not a fully authored four-direction asset set.

The app now applies a shared, alpha-preserving SVG colour matrix in `RoomItemArt.tsx` to soften the furniture into the surrounding walnut, oat and taupe palette. The same treatment is used on web, iOS and Android in the room, collection tray, shop and purchase dialog. These are display colours; the source PNG files and transparent padding are unchanged.

## Art-direction sheet prompt

Use case: product-mockup / stylized-concept. Create a carefully organised production art-direction sheet for BridgingMinds, a cosy learning app. Heading "My Space · The first furniture collection", subtitle "Fine pixels · Warm neutrals · Made to mix". White background, elegant readable charcoal sans-serif labels. At top left show a small empty isometric room shell with ivory walls and pale oak flooring labelled "Your room". The MAIN CONTENT is a spacious 4-column by 3-row catalogue of TWELVE clearly separated high quality pixel-art furniture assets, each with plenty of white breathing room, no touching objects. Labels exactly:
row1 "Oak desk", "Linen chair", "Oak shelf", "Woven rug".
row2 "Leafy plant", "Reading lamp", "Oatmeal sofa", "Coffee table".
row3 "Cushion stool", "Low cabinet", "Side table", "Ceramic plant".

ART STYLE: every asset precise fine pixel-art at roughly 96x96 to160x160 native sprite resolution enlarged cleanly, small crisp staircase edges, deliberate limited 6-shade material ramps, warm grey-brown outlines, no smooth vector curves, no sketchy painting, no tiny random noise, no glossy3D. Consistent orthographic 2:1 isometric camera looking from front-right above, verticals perfectly vertical; object front edges descend at exactly26.565degree; light from top left. Same physical scale: desk surface twice chair width, bookcase tall, floor lamp tall, sofa two seats, rug about3x3 floor cells. Palette cream #F5EEE4, pale oak #C5AB8C, walnut #7C6251, taupe #A59B8D, charcoalbrown #554B43, muted sage #889178. Very desaturated and soft but crisp contrast. Furniture polished and cute, cosy Singapore apartment reading nook, warm offwhite not yellow, no orange, no glitter and no painted scenery.

Oak desk: simple two-cell-wide pale oak writing desk with darker drawer on one side, EMPTY top so it can be modular.
Linen chair: squat oatmeal armchair, sage square cushion, compact onecellfootprint.
Oak shelf: freestanding tall walnut bookcase, neatly grouped cream/taupe/sage books.
Woven rug: flat light oatmeal rug, restrained taupe border, isometric diamond lying on the ground.
Leafy plant: tall leafy indoor plant, offwhite ribbed pot.
Reading lamp: slim freestanding brown floor lamp with a cream pleated shade.
Oatmeal sofa: matching two-seater oatmeal sofa, one sage and one taupe cushion.
Coffee table: low rectangular warm oak table with EMPTY top.
Cushion stool: round small linen padded stool on4oaklegs.
Low cabinet: low walnut two-door cabinet, EMPTY top.
Side table: small square oak occasional table, EMPTY top.
Ceramic plant: small lush tabletop-looking plant but freestanding on floor in muted cream round pot.

No characters, no room backdrop behind individual items, no overlapping assets, no product shadows extending beyond their local footprint. Tiny gentle ground contact shading only. It must read like a coherent handcrafted pixel-game asset kit, not a photo or interior moodboard. Footer "Asset preview · Starter furniture and optional Coin purchases".
