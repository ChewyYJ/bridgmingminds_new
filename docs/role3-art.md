# Role 3 scene art

Current Home, Discover, Street and Tournament scenes use the coordinated five-image set in `assets/role3/scenes/`. Exact prompts and provenance are recorded in [the scene asset manifest](../assets/role3/scenes/ASSETS.md). See [the polish implementation note](ui-polish-2026-11-16.md) for sizing and verification. The earlier source below is retained as historical artwork.

`assets/role3/debate-street.png` was generated with built-in ImageGen for this app. It contains no third-party logo, character or downloaded art. Existing avatars and food retain their provenance in `avatar-modular.md` and `avatar-food-assets.md`. The source remains unaltered; native layout crops it responsively.

## Exact generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a finished 2D side-scrolling game background for BridgingMinds Debate Street. Extra-wide landscape panorama, approximately 4.5:1 aspect ratio. Crisp fine pixel-art, tiny neat pixel clusters, warm cream, beige, taupe, brown outlines, muted sage trees, a few subdued terracotta and golden details. Match a refined cozy campus town, no chunky pixels or blur, no photorealism. Three districts across a single continuous horizontal walking pavement: left a small bookshop and bench beneath leafy trees, middle a friendly coffee shop and town noticeboard, right a handsome campus debate hall with stone steps, columns and a small golden trophy emblem. Buildings viewed side-on with a little depth; pale hazy city silhouettes far behind, white-to-ivory daytime sky. The bottom quarter must be a broad uninterrupted level stone-paved walking area, completely clear of characters and foreground obstacles. Walking ground near 82 percent height. Keep all architectural sizes consistent, detailed but calm. The scene fills the image edge-to-edge with no margins. No people, no avatars, no UI, no frames, no lettering, no signage text, no logos, no speech bubbles. Opaque background. This image will sit behind independently animated avatars and native interface controls.
