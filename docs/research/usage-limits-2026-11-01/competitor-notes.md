Research date: 1 November 2026. Official public pricing, support and terms only. These are published entitlements, not verified backend enforcement or measured unit economics. Dollar prices below are as displayed on the cited websites; Singapore checkout prices, tax and store offers were not checked. No accounts were created or paid services invoked.

## What “unlimited” does and does not tell us

Talkpal's terms, effective 20 October 2026, explicitly restrict duplicate accounts, repeat trials, sharing/reselling access, automated abuse and promotion/refund exploitation. It can limit or terminate violating use. The terms do **not** disclose a numerical monthly cap for ordinary Premium practice in the text inspected. Its direct-web refund offer permits a request within 30 days of a charge, once per person and subject to anti-abuse conditions; app-store refunds follow the relevant store. These facts show contractual controls alongside the unlimited offer, not proof of any particular hidden cap. [Talkpal terms, sections 7 and 9A](https://talkpal.ai/terms-and-conditions/).

Yoodli also supports administrator-configured roleplay time limits. Its August 2026 release notes specifically describe a hard limit. This is an organization content setting, **not** evidence that every individual subscription has the same duration ceiling. [Yoodli release notes](https://support.yoodli.ai/en/articles/9048809-yoodli-release-notes).

The public evidence does not reveal these vendors' model routing, cached-result savings, negotiated inference prices, actual usage distribution or gross margins. Do not infer that unlimited AI is cheap, that all competitors secretly cap usage, or that a given cap improves learning.

## Patterns to borrow

These are recommendations inferred from the comparison, not competitor facts or proven conversion results:

1. **Present a useful unit, not tokens.** Speech competitors use sessions, minutes or generated lessons. BridgingMinds can display AI feedback allowances, while tracking tokens and money internally.
2. **Separate practice from paid computation.** Preserve reading, writing, replay, reflection and peer practice when AI allowance is exhausted. Speak's feature-specific generation quota illustrates the distinction.
3. **Bound both frequency and size.** A session allowance needs a maximum recording duration, input length, debate turns and output length to control worst-case cost.
4. **Show the entitlement before the user invests effort.** Display what counts, allowance remaining, reset date/time and whether a revision costs another unit. A weekly balance gives students more scheduling flexibility than a rigid one-session-per-day ceiling, but must be tested with actual demand.
5. **Make service failures free to the learner.** Do not deduct another allowance for a duplicate click, failed provider response or retry of the same failed job. Do not promise unlimited automatic retry: reserve a separate internal retry budget.
6. **Avoid a lifetime unlimited AI commitment at launch.** Orai offers one, but the public evidence cannot establish its economics. Recurring compute costs make a finite allowance easier for this project to budget and explain.
7. **Do not mix garden currency with AI purchasing power.** Practice rewards should support the existing habit loop. Otherwise the user may consume paid AI to earn the currency needed to consume more AI, and exhaustion could threaten their plants.
8. **Prefer an explicit upgrade or prepaid pack to surprise overage charges.** No verified comparable above showed a token-priced consumer overage tariff on the inspected pages. This absence is not proof that add-ons never exist; it is a reason to keep the proposed user experience simple.

## Research boundaries

- This review did not inspect in-app paywalls, signed-in quotas, Singapore store SKUs, accessibility behavior or actual cancellation flows.
- No numerical “unlimited” threshold was verified for Yoodli Advanced, Talkpal Premium, Orai Pro, ELSA Premium, Speak Plus or Poised.
- Free-plan exact allowances were verified for Yoodli and Talkpal; Speak's **paid** generation feature cap was verified. ELSA free usage was only verified as limited, without a universal number.
- Older official ELSA FAQs contain different trial descriptions; the newer dated guide was preferred, with regional caveats retained.
- Refund summaries describe the provider's published policy; they are not legal advice or a conclusion about enforceability in Singapore.
