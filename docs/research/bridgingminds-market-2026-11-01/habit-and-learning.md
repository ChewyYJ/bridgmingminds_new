## Learning evidence and its limits

**Gamification:** Sailer and Homner's meta-analysis reported positive average effects in educational studies, with substantial variation. Motivational and behavioral results were less stable in the subset of higher-rigor studies. It does not validate this garden, daily streaks, or plant death. Practical inference: measure completed practice and return use separately from independent communication performance. [Original meta-analysis, published 2019; volume 2020](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10648-019-09498-w)

**Assisted output versus independent learning:** Bastani and colleagues studied nearly 1,000 students at one Turkish high school in mathematics. Access to GPT-based assistance improved practice results; the unrestricted interface's users subsequently performed worse without it. A tutor with safeguards mitigated that harm but did not establish an advantage over control on the unassisted exam. This study is not about Singapore adults, speaking practice, or today's model versions. It supports testing independent performance instead of treating a polished AI-assisted answer as evidence of learning. The publisher records an August 2025 correction concerning an affiliation. [Published paper and correction record](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2422633122)

**Pronunciation:** The Council of Europe's phonological-competence guidance places intelligibility ahead of resemblance to a native-speaker accent. Practical inference: any later pronunciation feature should assess whether speech can be understood in context, validate flags with representative speakers and listeners, and distinguish a recognition error from a learner error. This does not turn a CEFR-inspired feature into an accredited or validated assessment. [Council of Europe](https://www.coe.int/en/web/common-european-framework-reference-languages/phonological-competence)

## A small qualitative signal, not a market estimate

One public-speaking discussion describes a person who feels capable with a prepared speech but loses the thread when responding spontaneously. This is an illustration of a concrete job to investigate: organising a response while under time pressure. It is one self-selected account, with no verified Singapore affiliation, and cannot establish prevalence or willingness to pay. Relative posting dates differ between the search index and page, so only the access date is relied on. The replies contain product promotion and were not used to rank products. [Discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/PublicSpeaking/comments/1wth7b9/i_struggle_with_impromptu_speaking/)

## Product consequences to test

1. Make the user's response the lasting artifact: what they claimed, what supported it, what changed after a challenge, and what they could later do unaided.
2. Keep the optional AI model response distinguishable from the learner's own work. A fresh response without that model answer is a stronger learning check than copying its phrasing into a retry.
3. Let the garden acknowledge effort without implying that XP measures reasoning ability. Test whether care obligations cause useful return practice or merely resource maintenance and avoidance after a missed day.
4. Use one concrete practice invitation. The app already has many activities; breadth should not become another task for an uncertain new user.
5. Compare the complete experience with a well-prompted general AI and a user's actual existing routine. A pleasing garden alone is insufficient evidence of incremental educational or commercial value.

No user interviews, paid competitor subscriptions, or causal product trials were conducted for these notes.
