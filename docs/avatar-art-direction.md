# Illustrated avatar revision

The user's reference is the eight-screen BridgingMinds mockup supplied on 15 November 2026. The first procedural pack did not meet that reference. This revision uses illustrated raster artwork generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool, then crops and assembles those real painted pieces into compatible sprite layers. No image-generation API key or fallback CLI was used.

The editable master assets are in [`assets/avatar/illustrated-v2/source`](../assets/avatar/illustrated-v2/source/): `character-master.png`, `girl-kit.png`, `boy-kit.png`, `details-kit.png` and `pixel-faces-v3.png`. All five are ImageGen-created raster sources. The importer uses their alpha channels, suppresses near-transparent background noise, registers cells and samples transformed pieces with premultiplied-alpha interpolation. The details sheet supplies complete individual shoes for independent foot placement and six chibi hand-pose cells for expressive gestures. The fifth sheet replaces the facial artwork with simpler eyes and visible pixel contours; its exact generation prompt is in [Pixel face and attachment polish](avatar-pixel-polish.md).

## Direction

- Soft, cute anime illustration with warm cocoa outlines, cream highlights and natural cloth folds. The latest revision uses simpler pixel faces and a stronger pixel finish for the chibi.
- White presentation background; charcoal, taupe, cream, muted sage, denim and caramel clothing.
- Adults use human proportions and perform idle/blink and walking only.
- Chibi avatars are expressive upper-body busts. They have no hips, legs, trousers or shoes. They perform idle/blink, greeting, speaking, celebration and eating.
- Three designs per category in each separate boy/girl collection. A saved outfit controls both representations; bottoms and shoes appear only on the adult.

The current manifest version is `bridgingminds-illustrated-v5-meals`; the `illustrated-v2` asset directory and `artifacts/avatar-modular/index.html` preview location remain stable. Before packing the atlas, the compiler quantises every chibi layer on a common 3 px canvas grid and adult head-related layers, including rear accessories, on a 2 px grid. Expression features use a registered head silhouette, and the face, hair, hats and head accessories share a head transform. Rear straps sit behind the torso, hats cover head accessories, and a jeans-based waist reference keeps bottom proportions consistent. These controls address shape drift and mismatched attachment motion; visual review is still required after a source-art change. The [focused head-fit and food audit](avatar-modular.md#focused-head-fit-and-food-audit) follows up on the user's hair, headphone and glasses screenshots and checks the three distinct meals. The earlier wardrobe review remains limited to its recorded v4 hashes.

The meal actions use the separate ImageGen-created [food sources](avatar-food-assets.md) rather than substituting a label on the old ramen animation. One shared meal layout coordinates held artwork, hand positions and sleeve motion. The preview offers one Eat control with three food choices, independent of saved outfit data.

### Shoes and chibi hand details

**Production brief summary, not a verbatim generation prompt:** create a transparent, evenly spaced six-column by two-row illustrated parts sheet matching the existing warm, softly shaded avatar artwork. Keep every part complete and isolated, with no labels, background or completed characters. The first row contains six individual right-facing shoes, one per cell: an ivory chunky sneaker, cocoa penny loafer, taupe ankle boot, clean white casual sneaker, cocoa lace-up boot and charcoal loafer. Each cell contains one complete shoe rather than a pair, so the rig can position the feet independently. The second row contains six chibi gesture assets: an open waving hand, a left fist, a right fist, an upturned supporting palm, a hand holding chopsticks and a pair of relaxed hands. Use consistent peach skin, cocoa outlines, gentle highlights and readable fingers/wrists, with enough transparent gutter for safe extraction.

`details-kit.png` supplements the original boy/girl sheets without replacing them. These source assets and assembled previews have not established physical-device rendering, motion or performance validation; native verification remains separate release work.
