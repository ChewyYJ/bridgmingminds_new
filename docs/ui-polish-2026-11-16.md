# Scene, street and room polish

Implemented on `person3/home-feature` over the existing four-tab Home / Discover / Debate / My Space work.

- Replaced the narrow licensed-town compositions on Debate Street, Discover, Home progress and the tournament with five bundled, muted neighbourhood illustrations. Street has two distinct adjoining panels; all backgrounds cover their visible bounds. The wardrobe's native room backdrop is retained.
- Debate Street is now 1920 by 320 display units with six selectable neighbours: Mina, Leo, Ari, Safiya, Daniel and Kai. Each has a distinct outfit and discussion style. Walking, panning and neighbour shortcuts reach both ends; the camera follows the animated player.
- New Singapore Civic Cup challenges consist of one Chair Lin boss match. Opening, rebuttal and closing remain the three turns within that match. A judged win completes the cup, a draw retries the same boss, and a loss ends that challenge. Saved historical cups remain resumable and display only the current challenger. No-key mode remains explicitly unscored practice.
- Raised the room shell from a 0.8 to 1.02 height/width ratio without changing floor coordinates. A shared alpha-preserving SVG colour matrix softens every furniture sprite in the room, editor, shop and confirmation. Original source PNGs and placement bounds remain intact.
