# Pixel face and attachment polish

This revision keeps the separate wardrobes and the adult/chibi animation split. It corrects attachment registration and proportions while increasing the visible pixel finish, especially on the chibi.

The replacement head sheet was generated with built-in ImageGen and saved to [`pixel-faces-v3.png`](../assets/avatar/illustrated-v2/source/pixel-faces-v3.png). The seven columns are adult neutral, adult blink, chibi neutral, chibi blink, speaking, celebrating and eating. The two rows are girl and boy. The source sheet remains unchanged during deterministic sprite builds.
